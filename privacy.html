Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

LawnWorks ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through lawnworkslandscaping.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you submit our Request a Proposal form, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, property or company name, property address (if given), and any message you include.

We also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site, such as which pages are viewed and general location data. This is collected automatically and does not identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your proposal request and schedule a property walkthrough
  • To communicate with you about our services
  • To improve our website based on how it's used

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Form Submissions

Form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify, our website hosting provider, and are accessible only to LawnWorks. You can review Netlify's own privacy practices at netlify.com/privacy.

Cookies & Analytics

Our site uses Google Analytics, which may place cookies in your browser to collect anonymized usage data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using tools like the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Your Choices

You can ask us to delete any information you've submitted to us by emailing ryan@lawnworkslandscaping.com. We'll remove it from our records except where we're required to keep it for legal or accounting purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to ryan@lawnworkslandscaping.com or by calling (256) 415-6503.

This policy is a general description of our data practices and is not legal advice. If you have specific legal requirements, consider having this reviewed by an attorney.
